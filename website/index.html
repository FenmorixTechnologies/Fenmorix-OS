Not a public safety agency? Same system, your words.

Cities, parishes, businesses and schools use the same core with different modules switched on. Menus, job roles, pay periods and wording change to fit the organization, without a custom build.

Government

Requisitions with the signatures auditors expect, staff schedules and time, who's out, help desk and equipment across buildings, emergency notifications for storms and outages, and reports for the council or commission.

Business

Time clock and schedules, who's in today, purchase requests and expense claims with receipts, a help desk, shared logins only the right people can reveal, and screens for the front counter.

Education

Campus alerts and roll call for faculty and staff, staff forms and approvals, who's out before the first bell, classroom purchasing, teacher certifications, board reports, and a gradebook students and parents sign in to.
